ABOUT STALE LINKS (HTTP 403/429)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

These are EXPECTED and NOT a bug:

- momot.rs is a temporary mirror - URLs expire after time
- Anna's Archive rate limits mirrors (HTTP 429 Too Many Requests)
- When a book fails with 403/429, the URL is dead at that mirror

SOLUTION: Re-search for the book
  1. Clear search cache: rm -rf data/search_cache.json
  2. Search for the book title again
  3. Anna's Archive will return NEW/FRESH momot.rs links
  4. Try downloading again

WHY NOT PERMANENT CACHE?
  - Permanent cache would mean stale links forever
  - Removing cache means fresh searches = fresh links
  - This is the CORRECT behavior
