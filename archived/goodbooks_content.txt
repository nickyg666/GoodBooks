=== WELCOME ===
Welcome to GoodBooks!

GoodBooks is your personal ebook manager and Kindle delivery system. It's designed to keep your book collection organized, searchable, and instantly accessible from any device.

Whether you're managing a large library, searching for specific titles, or sending books to your Kindle, GoodBooks makes it simple and seamless.

=== QUICK START ===
Get Up and Running in Minutes

Step 1: Access Your Library
Open the GoodBooks web app from any browser. You'll see your entire ebook collection organized by title, author, and custom folders.

Step 2: Search and Browse
Use the search feature to find any book by title or author. Filter by genre, sort by date added or title, and browse your books however you prefer.

Step 3: Send to Kindle
Found a book you want to read? Click "Send to Kindle" and select your user account. The book is automatically formatted and sent to your Kindle device within seconds.

Step 4: Check History
Your reading history automatically tracks every download and delivery. Visit the History page anytime to see what you've sent and when.

=== FEATURES ===
What You Can Do with GoodBooks

Library Management: View all your ebooks in an organized grid. Each book shows the cover, title, and quick access buttons.

Smart Search: Search Anna's Archive for new books without leaving the app. Download directly or set up automatic delivery to your Kindle.

Feed Integration: Connect RSS feeds and HTML pages to automatically discover new books matching your interests.

One-Click Delivery: Select your Kindle email address and send any book with a single click. Formatting is automatic.

Reading History: Track every book you've downloaded and sent. See where each book came from and when you accessed it.

Custom Organization: Create folders and collections to organize your library however makes sense to you.

=== TIPS & TRICKS ===
Make the Most of GoodBooks

Batch Operations: Select multiple books at once and send them all to Kindle in one action.

Autodownload: When searching, enable autodownload to automatically fetch and send matching books to your Kindle.

Feed Monitoring: Set up feeds to automatically check for new books daily. Run feeds manually or let them run in the background.

Metadata Refresh: Let GoodBooks pull book information from Goodreads to enrich your library with ratings, genres, and descriptions.

Organize First: Take time to sort and organize your library. Use the folder view to create a structure that works for you.

=== SETTINGS ===
Customize Your Experience

Configure your Kindle email address in Settings.

Add multiple user accounts if you have different Kindles in your household.

Set up SMTP for automatic email notifications when books are sent.

Connect RSS feeds to discover new content automatically.

Adjust download folders and storage locations as needed.

=== GETTING HELP ===
Support

Most issues can be resolved by:

Checking the History page to verify your downloads

Refreshing metadata to get updated book information

Running feeds manually to check for new discoveries

Reviewing the Settings page to ensure your Kindle email is correct

All your books are safely stored and organized. Your Kindle email is encrypted and never shared.
